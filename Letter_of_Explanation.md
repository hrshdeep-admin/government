# Letter of Explanation — proposed v1.0
The repository is institutional memory, not the AI conversation. The Prime Minister retains approval and personal autonomy. Ministers advise independently within their charters. MVPRA coordinates strategy and time; MCR resolves disputes independently, with MCR–MVPRA disputes referred to the Prime Minister. MSRPT supplies evidence. The order below is proposed and requires ratification; it is not a ranking of personal importance.

## Files
AGENTS.md and this explanation are common, immutable during ordinary seasons. Each ministry has Prompt.md (appointment), Framework.md (searchable extraction of charter), Work_Log.md (append-only approved history), Current_Season.md (complete active policies), Intentions.md (frozen proposed direction). Original PDFs in Charters/ govern where ratified. Charter or protocol amendments require a separate explicit decision. Sensitive personal information stays outside GitHub, including raw journals, health records, bank records and private information about others.

## Monthly procedure
1. Prime Minister opens a season from the current main commit; create one season branch. Mark the phase Declaration in Parliament_Status.md; record baseline commit. MSRPT provides a short evidence summary first. Ministers declare independently against the same baseline and common evidence. Record only proposed changes, resource needs, constraints and affected policy IDs. Do not use other evolving declarations as a basis.
2. After all declarations are available (MCR may record no planned intervention), freeze Intentions.md for each ministry in one reviewed branch commit. Record its commit ID in Parliament_Status.md or task instructions; never amend frozen declarations in this season. Declarations are not policy.
3. Legislative order: MSRPT, MISD, MPMH, MVPRA, MoF, MFR, MKIG, MEESC. MCR acts only where a real dispute requires it. Each minister reads the frozen intentions and earlier provisional decisions. MVPRA considers all time requests; MoF considers budget requests and essential needs. MEESC coordinates execution last. Use `Parliament/Drafts/<code>.md` for provisional decisions; do not overwrite Current_Season.md or Work_Log.md during legislation. Publish each approved draft to the season branch before the next ministry begins; agents must verify they are reading the latest branch commit. If branch access fails, provide the relevant draft directly or halt; do not substitute main silently.
4. Give every policy a stable ID (e.g., MPMH-001) and revision. Declare dependencies by ID and revision. Do not rely on policies intended to be retired or amended by a later ministry; defer dependent proposals or make them explicitly optional and independent of uncertain outcomes. Unexpected conflicts go to MVPRA/MCR, with at most one targeted revision round; defer incompatible proposals or ask the Prime Minister. No recursive reviews. Urgent health/safety needs never wait for Parliament.
5. MVPRA checks resource feasibility, jurisdiction, dependencies and conflicts; MCR advises on disputed issues. Prime Minister reviews the complete package. On explicit approval, prepare a single final pull request: append each Work_Log.md, replace each Current_Season.md with all approved active policies (including unchanged ones), retain frozen Intentions.md, mark status Closed with season date, archive only concise decisions, and remove temporary drafts if no longer needed. Verify differences and merge manually. Do not infer approval or merge automatically. Keep prior history intact; corrections are new entries.

## Context discipline
Use a fresh task for each ministry/phase as desired. Only read the necessary files and relevant sections. Keep cross-ministry summaries short; use IDs rather than repeating long explanations. Record unknowns rather than guesses. Prefer no policy change where existing policy works. No artificial targets or rigid compliance requirements. These instructions are procedural, not enforced access controls; the Prime Minister reviews every proposed change.
